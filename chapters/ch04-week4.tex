This chapter adds \emph{names} to the calculator, and then \emph{functions}. Both are implemented by substitution. The real subject, though, is how subtle substitution turns out to be. Three questions come up again and again: when to evaluate the expression being substituted, how inner bindings shadow outer ones, and how a careless substitution can \emph{capture} a variable and change what a program means. The last section adds a \code{match} form to the same interpreter. This chapter covers:
\begin{itemize}
  \item \Cref{sec:letsubst}: \code{idC} and \code{letC}, eager substitution, and shadowing;
  \item \Cref{sec:lazy}: lazy substitution, its costs and benefits, and the variable-capture bug;
  \item \Cref{sec:capavoid}: capture-avoiding substitution with fresh names from \code{gensym};
  \item \Cref{sec:lambda}: \code{lambda} and application, a new \tyname{Value} type, eager and lazy application, and capture again, now also under eager evaluation;
  \item \Cref{sec:rec4}: Recitation~4, pattern matching in the interpreter.
\end{itemize}
